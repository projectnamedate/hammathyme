How it works

Three visits, one picture that flickers like it should.

Every head is stripped to its last screw, re-machined where it has worn, and timed on our bench before it leaves.

Restored

Lenses re-cemented and collimated, sprockets re-cut, the Geneva movement shimmed until it pulls each frame in a quarter turn.

Delivered

It arrives on its own cabinet with six features in cans, threaded and focused on your wall before we leave.

Spliced

A splicing kit comes in the cabinet, and a technician visits each season to clean the gate and check the loop.

Anatomy

150 parts, every one of them answerable.

  1. LampA carbon arc behind a condenser, re-fitted with a cool LED core that fits the original reflector.
  2. ShutterTwo blades: each frame is shown twice, 48 flashes a second, so the picture doesn't flicker.
  3. MovementA four-slot Geneva cross turns the 16-tooth sprocket a quarter turn per frame.
  4. GateThe aperture plate and pressure door, polished so the film rides on its edges, never its picture.
